## 3. About / Description (max 2,000 characters)

```
Likho turns handwriting practice into a happy adventure.

"Likho" means "write!" in Urdu. It is a mobile app for children aged 5 to 8 — KG to Class 3 — who are learning to read and write in English. With Pencil, a cheerful talking pencil, a child learns to write every letter of the alphabet: capital letters A to Z, then small letters and their sounds, then phonics, words, sentences and short stories.

HOW IT WORKS
• Trace, then join the dots, then write the letter alone — one letter at a time
• Likho checks every stroke and gives a gentle hint after a mistake
• Pencil reads every instruction out loud, so children who cannot read yet can play on their own
• 14 worlds and 210 levels, each one mixing writing with short games
• Stars, stickers, dress-up rewards and a certificate at the end of the course

WHY WE BUILT IT
Millions of children in Pakistan and across South Asia start school writing English before anyone has shown them how to hold the shape of a letter. Worksheets do not talk back, and a busy classroom cannot watch every stroke. Likho can: it listens to the hand, corrects kindly, and never runs out of patience.

FOR PARENTS
No ads. Up to 4 child profiles. A parent lock. Session limits of 5, 10 or 15 minutes. A clear view of which letters a child knows and which still need practice.

WHAT WE CARE ABOUT
Children first, and that means no ads, no dark patterns and no data we do not need. Craft over quantity: hand-made art, original music and a voice for every instruction. Learning that a parent can see, not just a score.

Likho is built in Pakistan for children everywhere.

Follow this page for progress on the app, the learning design behind it, and notes on building a kids product properly.
```
(1,724 characters.)
