# Likho Town · Episode A "Dada's Apple Garden" · image prompts (Gemini)

Made the same way as Pencil (design/mascot): one **master pose** first, then a
**2 × 4 expression sheet**, and things on a **grid sheet**, all on a plain
white background, 16:9. Backgrounds are phone-tall (9:16).

## How to use (every time)

1. Open a **new Gemini chat** for each character (so one character never
   leaks into another).
2. **Attach two pictures of Pencil** in that chat as the style reference:
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_ic1bzvic1bzvic1b.jpeg` (master pose)
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_y5imh7y5imh7y5im.jpeg` (expression sheet)
3. Paste the prompt. If the style drifts, say: *"Match the attached Pencil
   style exactly: same outline thickness, same soft shading, same eyes."*
4. For a character's expression sheet, stay in the **same chat** as their
   master pose and also attach the master you chose.
5. Save each picture with the **file name** given, in `design/town/a/`.
   I cut them out and put them in the app.

Rules for every picture (they are in the prompts too):
- outlines closed and unbroken (the background is cut away from the outside)
- nothing touching the picture's edge, space between poses
- no text, letters, numbers or watermark (the app writes the letters)

---

## The style (part of every prompt below)

> Style: match the attached Pencil mascot exactly. Cute 2D cartoon
> illustration for a children's learning app, clean vector look. Smooth,
> medium-thin outlines in a darker warm shade of each colour (never pure
> black), closed and unbroken. Soft warm colours with gentle gradient shading
> and one soft highlight, no texture. Rounded, friendly shapes. Big round
> dark-navy eyes with a small white shine dot, small pink blush on the cheeks,
> simple happy mouth. Not realistic, not 3D, not anime. No text, no letters,
> no numbers, no watermark.
