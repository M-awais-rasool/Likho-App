# Likho Games · "Monster Defense" · image prompts (Gemini)

Silly monsters come down the path from a dark cave to Pencil's garden.
Pencil traps them in magic bubbles, and when a bubble pops the monster turns
friendly and waves. Nobody gets hurt.

These are made the same way as the Stories art (design/town): first one **master
pose**, then a **pose sheet**, then backgrounds and things. Everything is on a
plain white background. Backgrounds are phone-tall (9:16).

The game is played in **portrait**. Monsters start small and far away at the
cave (top of the screen) and get bigger as they walk down to the
garden fence (bottom). That growing-closer feeling is the suspense. So all
monster poses face **towards the viewer**.

## How to use (every time)

1. Open a **new Gemini chat** for each monster, so one monster never
   leaks into another.
2. **Attach two pictures of Pencil** in that chat as the style reference:
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_ic1bzvic1bzvic1b.jpeg` (master pose)
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_y5imh7y5imh7y5im.jpeg` (expression sheet)
3. Paste the prompt. If the style drifts, say: *"Match the attached Pencil
   style exactly: same outline thickness, same soft shading, same eyes."*
4. For a monster's pose sheet, stay in the **same chat** as its master
   and also attach the master you chose.
5. Save each picture with the **file name** given, in `design/games/monster-defense/`
   (jpeg is fine, the name is what matters). I cut them out and put them in the app.

Rules for every picture (they are in the prompts too):
- outlines closed and unbroken (the background is cut away from the outside)
- nothing touching the picture's edge, space between poses
- no text, letters, numbers or watermark (the app writes the words)
- monsters are **cute-silly, never scary**: round, soft, small teeth at most,
  no blood, no claws, no weapons

The app adds these itself, so **do not** ask Gemini for them: the word
badges above the monsters, magic bubbles, magic beams, sparkles, dizzy stars,
hearts, score and buttons.

## The style (part of every prompt below)

> Style: match the attached Pencil mascot exactly. Cute 2D cartoon
> illustration for a children's learning app, clean vector look. Smooth,
> medium-thin outlines in a darker warm shade of each colour (never pure
> black), closed and unbroken. Soft warm colours with gentle gradient shading
> and one soft highlight, no texture. Rounded, friendly shapes. Big round
> dark-navy eyes with a small white shine dot. Not realistic, not 3D, not
> anime. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.
