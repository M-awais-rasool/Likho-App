# Likho Games · "Monster Smash" · image prompts (Gemini)

Angry Birds style. Pencil stands on a little hill on the left with a big
wooden slingshot. On the right, the silly monsters from Monster Defense
(Gloop, Fuzz, Bonk, Boo; we already have their pictures) sit on towers of
wooden, ice and stone blocks. Every block has a letter, which the app writes.
Pencil says "Hit the B!", the child pulls the slingshot back and lets go, the
ball flies, hits the B block, and the tower wobbles and falls; the monsters
tumble and turn into friends.

This game is played **sideways (landscape)**, like the real Angry Birds,
so the backgrounds are **wide (16:9)**.

The app draws these itself, so **do not** ask Gemini for them: the letters on
the blocks, the slingshot's rubber band, the aiming dots, cracks, dust,
splinters, stars, "POW!" words, score and buttons.

## How to use (every time)

1. Open a **new Gemini chat** for each picture group.
2. **Attach two pictures of Pencil** as the style reference:
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_ic1bzvic1bzvic1b.jpeg` (master pose)
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_y5imh7y5imh7y5im.jpeg` (expression sheet)
3. Paste the prompt. If the style drifts, say: *"Match the attached Pencil
   style exactly: same outline thickness, same soft shading, same colours."*
4. For a pose sheet, stay in the **same chat** as its master and also
   attach the master you chose.
5. Save each picture with the **file name** given, in
   `design/games/monster-smash/` (jpeg is fine, the name is what matters).

Rules for every picture (they are in the prompts too):
- outlines closed and unbroken (the background is cut away from the outside)
- nothing touching the picture's edge, clear space between things
- no text, letters, numbers or watermark

> black), closed and unbroken. Soft warm colours with gentle gradient shading
> and one soft highlight, no texture. Rounded, friendly shapes. Not realistic,
> not 3D, not anime. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.

2. `design/games/monster-defense/gloop_master.jpeg` (a monster)

The layout matters, because the game runs on top of it:
- **bottom 15%**: flat, even ground all across the picture (grass on top,
  earth below). Everything stands on it, so it must be **perfectly flat and
  straight**
- **left third**: open sky and space (Pencil and the slingshot stand there)
- **right two thirds**: open space above the ground (the towers stand there)
- the scenery (hills, trees, clouds) is only far away in the back, low and
  soft, never in front

If the picture comes out tall or square, reply: *"Make it a wide 16:9
landscape picture, same scene."*
