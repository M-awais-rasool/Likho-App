# Likho Games · "Monster Smash" · the Stationery Squad 2.0 · image prompts (Gemini)

The heroes that fly from the slingshot. They replace the old code-drawn
eraser, ink drop and crayon. Same names, same powers, a new look:

| Hero  | What it is                  | Power (tap while flying)       |
|-------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Rubby | a round pink eraser ball    | **Boing**: bounces hard        |
| Inky  | a round blue ink ball       | **Split**: becomes 3 small Inkys |
| Crayo | a round orange crayon ball  | **Dash**: shoots forward, breaks wood and ice |

What makes them good flying heroes (it is in every prompt):
- **Round.** The body is one chubby ball, so it reads well when small, spinning and flying.
- **Face first.** Big eyes, thick bold eyebrows, a big expressive mouth. The face carries the feeling.
- **One strong colour each.** The child can tell them apart even when they are tiny.
- **No arms, no legs.** Only two tiny feet, so they look good flying and squashing.
- **Facing right**, towards the towers, in a three-quarter view.

The app draws these itself, so **do not** ask Gemini for them: the trail
behind a flying hero, dust and "poof" clouds, stars, sparkles, the 3 small
Inkys (the app shrinks the Inky picture), the slingshot, the hero cards,
letters, numbers and buttons.

## How to use (every time)

1. Open a **new Gemini chat** for each hero.
2. **Attach three pictures** as the style reference:
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_ic1bzvic1bzvic1b.jpeg` (Pencil, master pose)
   - `design/mascot/Gemini_Generated_Image_y5imh7y5imh7y5im.jpeg` (Pencil, expression sheet)
   - `design/games/monster-defense/gloop_master.jpeg` (a monster from this game)
3. Paste the **master** prompt. Make it again until you love it. This is
   the most important picture.
4. In the **same chat**, attach the master you chose and paste the **pose
   sheet** prompt.
5. Save each picture with the **file name** given, in
   `design/games/monster-smash/heroes/` (jpeg is fine, the name is what matters).

If the style drifts, say: *"Match the attached Pencil style exactly: same
outline thickness, same soft shading, same colours."*

If a sheet's hero changes shape or colour, say: *"Keep the hero exactly like
the attached master: same body, same colours, same eyebrows, only the face
and squash change."*

Rules for every picture (they are in the prompts too):
- outlines closed and unbroken (the app cuts the white away from the outside)
- nothing touching the picture's edge, clear white space between poses
- no text, letters, numbers or watermark

## 1. Rubby (the leader: a round pink eraser ball)

### 1a · Master → `rubby_master.png`

```
Create a hero character for a children's slingshot game, in the same style
as the attached Pencil mascot and the attached monster.

small happy confident smile, soft pink blush on the cheeks.

No arms. Only two tiny rounded orange-pink feet peeking out at the bottom.

View: three-quarter view, the whole body facing to the RIGHT.

Pose: standing still on its tiny feet, chest out, proud and ready.

Style: match the attached Pencil mascot exactly. Cute 2D cartoon
illustration for a children's learning app, clean vector look, premium
mobile game quality. Smooth, medium-thin outlines in a darker warm shade of
each colour (never pure black), closed and unbroken. Rich, warm colours with
soft gradient shading, a gentle shadow on the lower right and one glossy
highlight on the upper left. Rounded, chubby, friendly shapes. Not
realistic, not 3D, not anime. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.

Composition: one character centred, plain pure white background, a soft
light-grey oval shadow under it, lots of empty space around it, nothing

```

### 1b · Pose sheet → `rubby_sheet.png`

```
Using the attached Rubby (keep it exactly the same: the round pink rubber
ball, the sky-blue paper band with the white stripe, the pink curl on top,
the thick eyebrows, the tiny feet, the same outline and shading), make a
pose sheet: 6 poses in 2 rows of 3, all the same size, all facing to the
RIGHT, evenly spaced with clear white space between them.

Top row, left to right:
1. idle: standing, calm happy smile, eyes open, looking right
2. aim: eyebrows pulled down hard, eyes narrowed and focused on something
   far to the right, mouth a tight determined line, body squashed a little
   lower and wider, ready to launch
3. flying: body stretched a little longer from left to right, eyes wide,
   eyebrows up, mouth wide open in an excited yell "Yaaah!", feet tucked in
   so they barely show

Bottom row, left to right:
4. power (boing): body squashed flat and very wide like a bouncing rubber
   ball, eyes squeezed shut, big grin
5. bonked: body squished and tilted, eyes dizzy (simple spirals), tongue
   out a little, eyebrows wobbly (funny, not hurt)
6. happy: hopping up, eyes closed in happy upside-down arcs, mouth open
   laughing, big pink blush

Style: match the attached Pencil mascot exactly, same outlines and soft
shading. Plain pure white background, a soft light-grey oval shadow under
each pose, nothing touching the edges. No text, no labels, no numbers.
16:9 image.
```

Open a **new Gemini chat**. Attach the Pencil pictures, **Rubby's sheet**

idle in the line, aim in the slingshot, flying in the air, power on tap,
bonked when it lands, happy when a letter is found. The app adds the
squash and stretch, the spin, the trail and the dust. The heroes will also
